Final brand direction

Warm enough to feel human. Precise enough to feel native.

Cue should feel like a beautifully engineered Mac writing instrument: private, fast, exact, and quiet. The brand is warm without becoming soft, premium without feeling luxurious for its own sake, and opinionated without reading as severe.

Foundation

What the brand should communicate

Positioning

Premium indie Mac utility. Not a chatbot, not a collaboration suite, not a feature pile.

Personality

Calm, competent, editorial, and highly opinionated. A little warmth, zero hype.

Emotional read

Trustworthy, quiet, and immediate. It should feel ready before the user even thinks about it.

Palette

Warm neutrals with one signal color

Most of the interface should feel like paper, metal, and ink. The accent exists to denote readiness and action.

Paper #F5F0E6
Mist #E5DDCF
Ink #171816
Signal #D26B3C

System

Editorial hierarchy, native utility behavior

Typography

Use a refined serif for headlines and a clean native-feeling sans for interface text. Marketing gets authority; product stays legible and direct.

  • DisplayIowan / Canela-like editorial serif
  • UISF Pro / Avenir Next / native grotesk
  • ToneShort, declarative, and precise

Graphic language

The visual metaphor is simple: waveform to cursor. Build around thin dividers, rounded cards, subtle glows, and sparse motion rather than decorative spectacle.

  • ShapesSoft rectangles and deliberate radii
  • MotionPulse, settle, then disappear
  • MaterialsWarm glass and paper-like surfaces
