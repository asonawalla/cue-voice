# Decisions

## 2026-08-05: Cue is one fixed Parakeet dictation loop

Cue keeps one system-wide workflow: hold Option-Space, speak, release, and paste one FluidAudio/Parakeet transcript into the application that was active on key-down. The model, shortcut, and output are fixed. Streaming preview, settings, history, diagnostics, clipboard restoration, alternate engines, and other optional surfaces are removed rather than hidden.
